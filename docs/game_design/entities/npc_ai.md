# NPC AI System

## 1. Purpose
Define the behavior, memory, and agency of Non-Playable Characters (NPCs) to make them feel like real, independent actors within the Survival Road world.

## 2. Responsibilities
- Manage NPC daily routines, goals, and survival needs.
- Handle conversation trees and dynamic dialogue generation via AI prompts.
- Track NPC inventory, combat capability, and vehicle status.

## 3. Core Attributes
Every major NPC must have:
- **Personality Archetype**: (e.g., Cynical Survivor, Naive Optimist, Ruthless Trader).
- **Goals**: Short-term (find water) and Long-term (reach the sanctuary).
- **Likes/Dislikes**: Affects conversation outcomes and gifts.
- **Secrets**: Hidden information unlocked via high Trust.
- **Combat Ability**: Stats mapping to the same rules as the player.

## 4. AI Orchestration
- NPC dialogue is not hardcoded. It is generated by the `NPC Prompt Module`.
- The prompt is injected with: The NPC's state, current environment, recent player actions, and relationship values.
- **Memory Compression**: Interactions are summarized by a background AI process and stored in the NPC's `Long-Term Memory` block to maintain continuity across encounters.

## 5. Agency & Survival
- NPCs are not static. They travel the road, fight monsters, and scavenge. 
- An NPC can die if they encounter a Danger Level they cannot handle. The player can find their wrecked vehicle and loot their corpse.
- NPCs will actively trade with the player or attempt to rob them, depending on their archetype and relationship.

## 6. Edge Cases
- **Player Attacks NPC**: Instantly sets Trust/Affection to -100. NPC fights back or flees.
- **NPC Death Logic**: If a critical quest NPC dies, the quest fails or branches into a "recover the data" objective. No NPC is essential; the game must continue.

## 7. Dependencies
- Relies on: `relationship_system`, `combat_system`, `ai_orchestration`, `exploration`.

## 8. Persistence Requirements
- NPC state (Location, Stats, Memory Summary, Alive/Dead status) must be saved globally.
